Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: 5 May 2026

The corporate website is not transactional.

The Negbeta corporate website at negbeta.com does not sell products, take payments, or accept orders. There is therefore nothing on this website to refund or cancel.

Refunds and cancellations are governed by each individual product.

Negbeta Limited operates a portfolio of consumer products. Refund and cancellation terms are specific to the product the customer purchased and live on that product’s own website, app store listing, or in-app billing settings. To request a refund or cancel a subscription, please refer to the product through which the purchase was made:

  • For purchases made on a Negbeta product’s website, refund and cancellation requests are handled through that product’s in-app account settings or by contacting that product’s support team.
  • For purchases made through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, refund requests are handled by the respective platform under its own refund rules.

Statutory rights are unaffected.

Nothing in this policy or in any product-specific policy limits the statutory rights you may have under UK consumer protection law, including the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, where they apply.

Contact

If you cannot identify which Negbeta product you purchased through, or you cannot reach the relevant product’s support team, you can contact us at info@negbeta.com and we will direct you.
