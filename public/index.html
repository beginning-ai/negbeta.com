01 What we build

Voice‑first conversational AI, built end to end.

Most AI products today are text in, text out. Negbeta’s products are voice native: a user opens an app, speaks, and gets a real time spoken response from an animated character on screen. We build the model that drives the conversation, the renderer that animates the character, the safety system that keeps it on the rails, and the consumer apps that surface them.

  • Voice‑native models. Real time speech to speech, not text to speech glued onto a chat model.
  • Animated characters. Browser based 2D rendering with audio driven lip sync, expression and idle motion.
  • In house safety stack. A multi family content classifier covering crisis, harmful or policy violating content, and other moderation categories.
